HabitHub Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

The short version: HabitHub collects nothing. Everything you put into the app stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, and the app makes no network requests.

What data HabitHub stores

Your Habits, schedules, check-ins, streaks, Freezes, and reminder times are saved in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself (see Backups below). We have no servers and no way to see, access, or recover your data.

Backups

You can export a backup file of your data from Settings. The export uses the standard iOS share sheet — where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email, etc.) is entirely your choice, and it is governed by the privacy policy of whatever service you send it to. Importing a backup reads a file you pick and restores it on-device. HabitHub itself never transmits the backup anywhere.

Reminders

If you set a Reminder for a Habit, HabitHub asks for permission to send local notifications. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device by iOS. No push-notification service is involved, and no data is sent off the device.

Data we collect

None. No personal information, no usage analytics, no crash reports, no identifiers, no location. The App Store privacy label for HabitHub is “Data Not Collected,” and this page is the commitment behind it.

Children

HabitHub does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of HabitHub ever changes any of the above — for example, by adding optional sync — this policy will be updated first, and the change will be called out in the App Store release notes for that version.
