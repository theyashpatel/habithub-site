HabitHub Support

HabitHub is a habit tracker built around one idea: a streak that stays motivating because it can break, but won't collapse over a single bad day.

Get help

Email yashpatel26@gmail.com and I'll get back to you as soon as I can. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened — it makes fixes much faster.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your device, and nowhere else. Use Settings → Export backup to save a copy, and Import to restore it on a new phone.

Why didn't my streak break? You may have a Freeze protecting the missed day, or the day wasn't a Scheduled Day for that Habit — Rest Days never count against you.

I did the habit but forgot to tick it. Tap the day in the history strip — recent days can be backfilled without spending a Freeze.
